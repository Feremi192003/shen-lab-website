About

Computational molecular science

The Shen Laboratory is based in the Department of Pharmaceutical Sciences at the University of Maryland School of Pharmacy.

Our work brings together computational chemistry, biophysics, molecular simulation, and data science to investigate biological systems, therapeutics, and biomolecular materials.

Constant-pH molecular dynamics

Methods for modeling dynamic protonation and pH-dependent molecular behavior.

pKa calculations

Prediction of ionization states and electrostatic effects in proteins and ligands.

Receptors and transporters

Molecular mechanisms of recognition, signaling, transport, and conformational change.

Drug design

Simulation and data-science approaches for understanding targets and guiding inhibitor design.

Biomolecular materials

Molecular principles underlying responsive materials, self-assembly, and hydrogels.
